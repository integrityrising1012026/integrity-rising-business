# Integrity Rising — Sites Setup & Integration Guide

Complete step-by-step guide to connect WordPress, Gumroad, Systeme.io, and community.

---

## Overview: The Ecosystem

```
integrityrising.wordpress.com (Hub)
    ↓
    ├→ Gumroad (Sell workbooks)
    ├→ Systeme.io (Email + sales funnels)
    ├→ Discord/Circle (Community)
    └→ Feedback form (Testimonials + suggestions)
```

All platforms work together to:
1. **Attract** visitors to WordPress
2. **Convert** with low-ticket workbooks on Gumroad
3. **Nurture** with email on Systeme.io
4. **Engage** in community chat
5. **Iterate** with feedback and suggestions

---

## Phase 1: WordPress Setup (integrityrising.wordpress.com)

### Step 1: Core Pages

Create these pages in WordPress and add to main navigation:

**1. Home / Landing**
- Hero section: "Rise with Integrity"
- Who it's for (pain points)
- What you get (workbooks, courses, community)
- Social proof (testimonials, review count)
- Clear CTA: "Get Started" → Gumroad or email signup

**2. About**
- Founder story (why you started this)
- Mission and values
- What makes Integrity Rising different
- Photo and personal touch

**3. Workbooks**
- Product grid showing all workbooks
- Category filters (by topic)
- Price and short description
- "View on Gumroad" button
- Customer reviews/ratings

**4. Courses**
- Course descriptions
- "Enroll Now" buttons (link to Gumroad or Systeme.io)
- Course curriculum preview
- Student testimonials

**5. Free Resources**
- Free workbook sample (lead magnet)
- Free 7-day challenge signup
- Blog post directory
- Links to community

**6. Community**
- What the community is about
- Who can join
- Community values
- "Join Us" button (link to Discord/Circle invite)

**7. Share Your Ideas**
- Embedded feedback form
- Leaderboard of most-requested topics
- Testimonials from community
- "Submit Your Idea" CTA

**8. Testimonials**
- Customer stories
- Video testimonials (if available)
- Star ratings
- Before/after quotes

**9. Blog**
- Weekly motivational articles
- Educational posts linked to workbooks
- SEO-optimized titles
- Share buttons to social media

**10. Contact**
- Simple contact form
- Business email
- Social links
- Response time expectation

### Step 2: WordPress Plugins & Setup

Install these free or affordable plugins:

- **Jetpack** — SEO, sharing, analytics
- **WPForms** or **Gravity Forms** — Feedback form
- **Mailchimp** or **ConvertKit** — Email signup integration
- **Yoast SEO** — Search optimization
- **Elementor** (free) — Easy page building if not using WordPress.com theme

### Step 3: WordPress Design & Branding

- Choose a clean, professional WordPress.com theme
- Add logo and brand colors
- Create a branded header image
- Add social media links in footer
- Enable comments on blog posts (for engagement)

### Step 4: Initial Content

Publish before soft launch:

- 2 motivational blog posts
- 1 educational post (linked to your first workbook topic)
- Homepage with clear value proposition
- About page with founder story
- Community welcome page

## Phase 2: Gumroad Setup

### Step 1: Create Gumroad Account

1. Go to [gumroad.com](https://gumroad.com)
2. Sign up with email
3. Complete profile (name, bio, profile image)
4. Add bank account for payouts

### Step 2: Upload First Workbook

**Product Details:**
- **Title:** "Who Am I? — Self-Discovery Workbook"
- **Description:** 
  - What it's about (1–2 sentences)
  - Who it's for
  - What's inside (bullet points)
  - What they'll gain
- **Price:** $7–$17 (start low to test)
- **Cover image:** Professional PDF cover (Canva template)
- **Product file:** Upload PDF
- **License terms:** Personal use only

**Gumroad Settings:**
- Enable digital product delivery
- Enable customer messaging (optional)
- Add product to a collection: "Workbooks"
- Add product tags: "self-discovery", "workbooks", "personal-growth"

### Step 3: Create Product Collections

Organize by type:

- **Workbooks** (all individual workbooks)
- **Bundles** (multi-product discounts)
- **Courses** (video + workbook)
- **Challenges** (7–30 day programs)

### Step 4: Create Bundles

**Starter Bundle Example:**
- Products: Who Am I + Values Clarity + Positive Mindset
- Price: $27 (vs. $51 if bought separately)
- Title: "Integrity Rising Starter Pack"
- Description: Complete introduction to personal growth

### Step 5: Setup Follow-Up

In Gumroad product settings:
- Enable post-purchase message with:
  - Thank you
  - Instructions for using the workbook
  - Link to free community
  - Discount code for next purchase
  - Link to feedback form
  - Link to email signup

### Step 6: Connect to WordPress

Add to WordPress:
- Product grid showing Gumroad products (embed or link)
- "Buy on Gumroad" buttons to each product page
- Affiliate links (if using Gumroad affiliate program)

## Phase 3: Systeme.io Setup

### Step 1: Create Systeme.io Account

1. Go to [systeme.io](https://systeme.io)
2. Sign up (14-day free trial, then $25–$297/month plans)
3. Choose the plan that fits your needs (start with basic)

### Step 2: Build Email List

**Signup Form:**
- Add to WordPress sidebar
- Create a landing page on Systeme.io
- Offer free lead magnet: "Who Am I? Quick Start" PDF
- Collect name + email

**Systeme.io Settings:**
- Create audience/contact list
- Add form to WordPress homepage
- Enable double opt-in (confirmation email)

### Step 3: Welcome Email Sequence

Create 5-email welcome sequence (auto-sent after signup):

**Email 1 (immediate):** Welcome + Lead Magnet
- Subject: "Welcome to Integrity Rising — Here's Your Free Workbook"
- Deliver free PDF
- Share your story (1–2 sentences)
- Introduce the community

**Email 2 (Day 1):** Get Started
- Subject: "Your First Step: Who Am I?"
- Explain how to use the workbook
- 1 free exercise to try today
- Link to community

**Email 3 (Day 3):** Share Your Progress
- Subject: "How's Your Journey Going?"
- Encourage reflection
- Offer community support
- No hard sell

**Email 4 (Day 5):** Introduce First Product
- Subject: "You're Ready for the Next Step"
- Recommend Values Clarity Workbook ($17)
- Explain why it pairs with Who Am I
- Special 20% discount link to Gumroad

**Email 5 (Day 7):** Community Invitation
- Subject: "Join Our Community (Free)"
- Invite to Discord/Circle
- What to expect in community
- Encourage testimonials/feedback

### Step 4: Setup Sales Funnel (Optional)

If using Systeme.io to sell courses:
- Create product page in Systeme.io
- Add checkout (Systeme.io handles payments)
- Setup post-purchase automation
- Send product files and welcome sequence

Recommended: Use Gumroad for now (simpler), upgrade to Systeme.io courses later.

### Step 5: Setup Email Campaigns

**Weekly Motivational Email:**
- Send every [day of week]
- Subject: Motivational quote or daily wisdom
- Body: Short reflection + actionable tip
- CTA: Join community or check out workbooks

**Monthly Announcement Email:**
- New workbook launch
- Community wins/testimonials
- Most-requested topics
- Special discount for subscribers

### Step 6: Integration with WordPress

- Add Systeme.io signup form to WordPress
- Link from WordPress to Systeme.io landing pages
- Auto-add Gumroad buyers to email list (via Zapier integration if available)

## Phase 4: Community Setup (Discord or Circle)

### Choose Your Platform

**Discord (Free)**
- Pros: Free, easy, familiar to many
- Cons: Less structured, harder to monetize
- Best for: Starting out, high engagement

**Circle ($39–$99/month)**
- Pros: Professional, structured, includes courses and events
- Cons: Paid, learning curve
- Best for: Serious membership, premium feel

**Start with Discord, upgrade to Circle later.**

### Step 1: Create Discord Server

1. Go to [discord.com](https://discord.com)
2. Create account and new server: "Integrity Rising"
3. Create channels:

```
CATEGORIES & CHANNELS:

📌 WELCOME
  ├─ #welcome (pinned rules + how to get started)
  ├─ #introductions (members introduce themselves)
  └─ #announcements (product launches, events)

💪 COMMUNITY
  ├─ #wins-and-reflections (share progress)
  ├─ #accountability (weekly check-ins)
  ├─ #questions (ask for advice)
  └─ #general-chat (off-topic chat)

📚 WORKBOOKS & COURSES
  ├─ #who-am-i-workbook (specific discussions)
  ├─ #values-clarity-workbook
  ├─ #confidence-rising-workbook
  └─ #course-discussions

💡 FEEDBACK & IDEAS
  ├─ #feature-requests (suggest new products)
  ├─ #testimonials (share your story)
  ├─ #reviews (product feedback)
  └─ #bugs-and-issues (problems to fix)

🎯 CHALLENGES & EVENTS
  ├─ #7-day-challenge (current challenge)
  ├─ #upcoming-events (webinars, group calls)
  └─ #challenge-wins (celebrate completions)

📞 SUPPORT
  ├─ #help (how to use community)
  ├─ #how-to-use-workbooks (instructions)
  └─ #direct-messages-welcome (open DMs)
```

### Step 2: Create Welcome Message

Pinned message in #welcome:

```

We're a community of people rising with integrity, building confidence, 
and living with purpose.

👋 NEW HERE? INTRODUCE YOURSELF:

  • One goal you're working toward

📚 USING A WORKBOOK?
→ Join the channel for that workbook and ask questions:
  • #who-am-i-workbook

  • #confidence-rising-workbook

💡 HAVE AN IDEA?
→ Suggest new workbooks in #feature-requests
→ Share a testimonial in #testimonials

🤝 COMMUNITY GUIDELINES:
✅ Be respectful and encouraging
✅ Celebrate each other's wins
✅ Ask for support when you need it
✅ Share experiences without pressuring others

Let's rise together!
```

### Step 3: Setup Community Automation

- Welcome new members with automated message
- Create roles: Members, Beta Testers, Moderators
- Set up scheduled messages (daily motivational quote, weekly prompt)
- Pin important announcements

### Step 4: Connect to WordPress

Add to WordPress:
- Discord invite link (public)
- Embed Discord widget on community page (optional)
- Add "Join Community" buttons throughout site
- Share Discord link in Gumroad follow-up
- Add Discord link to Systeme.io emails

### Step 5: Monthly Community Events

- **Weekly Accountability Check-in** (Monday)
- **Monthly Group Call** (video or audio)
- **Challenge Kickoff** (first of month)
- **Testimonial Spotlight** (share customer stories)

## Phase 6: Integration Points

### WordPress → Gumroad
- Product cards with "View on Gumroad" links
- Workbook landing pages
- Blog posts recommending products

### WordPress → Systeme.io
- Email signup forms on every page
- Lead magnet download link
- Email opt-in for blog subscribers

### WordPress → Community
- "Join Community" button on every page
- Community highlights on homepage
- Member testimonials embedded

### Gumroad → Systeme.io
- Post-purchase email adds to Systeme.io list (if integrated)
- Upsell to email list in Gumroad follow-up

### Gumroad → Community
- Post-purchase message: "Join our community"
- Discount code for community members
- Feedback form link in follow-up

### Systeme.io → Gumroad
- Email campaigns link to Gumroad products
- Email sequences drive traffic to workbooks

### Systeme.io → Community
- Email invites subscribers to Discord
- Exclusive email-only community perks

### Community → Feedback Form
- Pinned channel for suggestions
- Weekly prompt: "What should we create next?"

### Feedback → Product Development
- Use suggestions to plan new workbooks
- Create based on community requests
- Beta test with community

## Monthly Maintenance

- **WordPress:** 2 new blog posts (4 hours)
- **Gumroad:** Monitor sales, respond to questions (1 hour)
- **Systeme.io:** Send weekly/monthly emails (2 hours)
- **Community:** Daily moderation, weekly events (5 hours)
- **Feedback:** Review submissions, update leaderboard (2 hours)

**Total monthly time: ~14–16 hours**

---

## Success Metrics (Track Monthly)

- WordPress: Visitors, email signups, bounce rate
- Gumroad: Sales, revenue, refunds, reviews
- Systeme.io: Email subscribers, open rate, click rate
- Community: Member count, active users, messages
- Feedback: Submissions received, ideas voted, testimonials published
- Overall: Revenue, customer acquisition cost (CAC), customer lifetime value (LTV)
