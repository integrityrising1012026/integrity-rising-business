## HTML Embedded Form

Add this form to WordPress using a form builder like:
- **Gravity Forms**
- **WPForms**
- **Formstack**
- **Typeform** (embedded)

Or use **Google Forms** and embed it directly.

### Form Fields

```
FORM TITLE: Share Your Ideas with Integrity Rising

FIELD 1: Submission Type (Required)
Type: Dropdown/Multiple Choice
Options:
  - Testimonial (share your story)
  - Feature Request (suggest a topic)
  - Product Review (review a workbook/course)
  - Question or Issue (get help)
  - General Feedback (anything else)

FIELD 2: Your Name or Display Name (Optional)
Type: Short Text
Placeholder: "What should we call you?"

FIELD 3: Your Email (Required — for follow-up)
Type: Email
Placeholder: "your@email.com"

FIELD 4: What Product Does This Relate To? (Optional)
Type: Dropdown/Multiple Choice
Options:
  - Who Am I? Workbook
  - Values Clarity Workbook
  - Positive Mindset Workbook
  - Confidence Rising Workbook
  - 90-Day Planner
  - Community
  - General Topic
  - Other

FIELD 5: Your Message (Required)
Type: Long Text / Textarea
Placeholder: "Tell us your idea, story, or feedback..."
Min characters: 20

FIELD 6: Rating (Optional — for reviews)
Type: Star Rating or Dropdown
Scale: 1–5 stars
Label: "How would you rate this product?"

FIELD 7: Would You Recommend This? (Optional)
Type: Yes/No
Label: "Would you recommend this to a friend?"

FIELD 8: Permission to Share (Conditional)
Type: Checkbox (appears if Testimonial or Review selected)
Label: "You can share my feedback publicly (with or without my name)"
Required: Checkbox confirmation

FIELD 9: How to Contact You (Optional)
Type: Dropdown
Options:
  - Email is fine
  - Only if you have questions
  - No contact needed

FIELD 10: Subscribe to Updates (Optional)
Type: Checkbox
Label: "Email me when new workbooks launch based on my suggestion"
```

### Form Settings

- **On submit:** Send confirmation email to user + admin notification
- **Redirect:** Thank you page with option to join community
- **Auto-reply:** "Thanks for your feedback! We read every submission and use them to build new products."

## Monthly Review Process

**Every first Monday of the month:**

1. Review all submissions from the past month
2. Count votes and prioritize by frequency
3. Approve testimonials and reviews for publication
4. Respond to questions and issues personally
5. Update the "Most-Requested Topics" leaderboard
6. Send monthly email: "Here's What You Asked For — Here's What We're Building"
7. Announce which topic will become the next workbook/course
