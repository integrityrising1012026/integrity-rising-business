# Integrity Rising — Business Blueprint

## Mission
Empower people to rise with integrity through practical workbooks, self-paced courses, motivational content, and a supportive community.

## Brand Promise
**Rise with Integrity. Lead with Purpose.**

## Business Model
Integrity Rising is a digital personal-growth platform offering:

- Printable and fillable workbooks
- Self-paced courses with workbook companions
- Motivational blog and email content
- Customer community and accountability challenges
- Reviews, testimonials, and customer-driven product ideas

## Platform Roles

| Platform | Role | Primary action |
|---|---|---|
| WordPress — `integrityrising.wordpress.com` | Public home, blog, product directory, testimonials, and suggestions | Visit, learn, join, and submit feedback |
| Gumroad | Digital product checkout and file delivery | Buy and download workbooks/courses |
| Systeme.io | Email list, funnels, automation, and course/customer follow-up | Subscribe, receive onboarding, and progress to the next offer |
| Community chat | Customer connection, accountability, support, and product discussions | Participate, share wins, and suggest ideas |

## Initial Product Path

### Foundation Collection
1. **Who Am I? — Self-Discovery Workbook**
2. **Values Clarity — Discover What Matters Most**
3. **Positive Mindset — Change the Way You Think**
4. **Confidence Rising — Build Self-Belief**
5. **90-Day Personal Growth Planner**

### Suggested pricing

- Individual workbook: $7–$17
- Premium workbook or planner: $17–$27
- Workbook bundle: $29–$49
- Self-paced course: $49–$199
- Challenge: $7–$17
- Optional membership: $9–$19/month

Start with one or two workbooks, validate demand, and build additional products from customer feedback.

## Website Structure

- **Home:** What Integrity Rising is and who it helps
- **About:** Founder story, values, and mission
- **Workbooks:** Links to Gumroad products grouped by topic
- **Courses:** Course descriptions and enrollment links
- **Free Resources:** Samples, articles, and challenges
- **Community:** Who it is for, expectations, and join link
- **Testimonials:** Approved customer stories and reviews
- **Suggest an Idea:** Customer form for topics, workbooks, and courses
- **Contact:** Support and business inquiries

## Community Structure

Recommended channels or sections:

- **Welcome:** Rules, orientation, and how to get started
- **Introductions:** Members share their goals
- **Wins and Reflections:** Progress and encouragement
- **Workbook Discussions:** Questions and practical exercises
- **Accountability:** Weekly check-ins
- **Suggestions:** New workbook and course ideas
- **Testimonials:** Customer stories and permission requests
- **Announcements:** New products, events, and updates

Community guidelines:

- Be respectful and encouraging.
- Do not provide medical, legal, or financial advice.
- Protect personal information.
- Share experiences without pressuring others.
- Report harmful or inappropriate content.
- Clarify that personal-growth materials are educational and are not a substitute for professional care.

## Feedback System

Create one simple form and link it from WordPress, Gumroad follow-up emails, Systeme.io, and the community.

Collect:

- Name or display name — optional
- Email — optional for follow-up
- Feedback type: review, testimonial, idea, question, or issue
- Product or course name
- Rating — optional
- What helped most?
- What should be improved?
- What topic should Integrity Rising create next?
- Permission to publish the submission — required checkbox

Moderate submissions before publishing. Do not publish private information without explicit permission.

## 30-Day Launch Plan

### Week 1 — Foundation

- Confirm the first two workbook topics.
- Create consistent branding, colors, and cover templates.
- Draft WordPress Home, About, Workbooks, Community, and Suggestions pages.
- Decide which community chat platform will be the customer home.

### Week 2 — Products and systems

- Finish the first workbook and a free sample.
- Create Gumroad product pages and test downloads.
- Create a Systeme.io signup form and welcome sequence.
- Add links between WordPress, Gumroad, Systeme.io, and the community.

### Week 3 — Community and content

- Publish community rules and welcome message.
- Add the suggestions/reviews/testimonial form.
- Publish two helpful WordPress articles.
- Invite a small beta group to test the experience.

### Week 4 — Soft launch

- Announce the first workbook.
- Offer a limited launch bundle or founding-customer discount.
- Ask beta customers for feedback and testimonials.
- Review traffic, sales, email signups, and community activity.
- Improve the product before expanding the catalog.

## Content Plan

Weekly minimum:

- 1 motivational article
- 1 educational article connected to a workbook topic
- 3 short social posts
- 1 email with encouragement, a practical exercise, and one clear call to action
- 1 community prompt

Every product should link to:

- A related free article
- One recommended next workbook or course
- The community
- The feedback form

## Metrics

Track monthly:

- WordPress visitors and email signups
- Gumroad views, sales, refunds, and conversion rate
- Systeme.io open and click rates
- Community members and active participants
- Reviews and testimonials received
- Suggestions by topic
- Revenue and expenses

## Responsible Business Practices

- Use original or properly licensed text, images, fonts, and music.
- Add clear refund, privacy, and contact information.
- Obtain permission before publishing testimonials.
- Keep customer data private and collect only what is needed.
- Include a personal-development disclaimer on relevant products.
- Verify current platform fees, tax requirements, and legal obligations before launch.
